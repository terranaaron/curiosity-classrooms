Curiosity Classrooms — four directions for the homepage

Four creative-direction prototypes for the Curiosity Classrooms website. Options A–C each speak to a single reader; Option D folds all three onto one page and adds a researched service menu — named packages with the deliverables each engagement produces. Same facts on every page — what changes is the voice, the look, and what the page asks the reader to do.

These are working drafts for review, not a live site. Dashed amber boxes mark facts and details still to be confirmed. Each page adapts to light or dark mode.

Option A · warm & energetic

For science teachers

Speaks to the teacher in the room: what a curiosity-driven lesson changes on an ordinary Tuesday, and an easy way to nudge their science lead or principal.

View option A
Option B · executive brief

For superintendents

A restrained, board-room read: division-level outcomes, a three-phase engagement model, and a briefing request — built for a thirty-second credibility check.

View option B
Option C · peer to peer

For division science leads

First person, colleague to colleague: a thought partner who has sat in the coordinator's chair, with a roadmap of ways to work together.

View option C
Option D · unified + service menu

For all three readers

One homepage with a door for each audience — plus the piece the other options don't have: a concrete service menu with named packages, and the deliverables the client keeps from each engagement.
