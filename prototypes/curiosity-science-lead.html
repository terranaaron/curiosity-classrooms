What I mean by a curiosity classroom

A classroom where students do science — where the practices, the crosscutting concepts, and the core ideas show up together because students are investigating something they actually want to figure out.

You already know the research. The gap is implementation at division scale: PD that changes Tuesday's lesson, materials that support inquiry instead of fighting it, and teachers who feel backed rather than evaluated.

That gap is the whole business.
