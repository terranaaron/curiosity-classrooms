The service menu

Every engagement is scoped to your division's size, calendar, and budget — but each one ends with named deliverables you keep. Chips show who typically brings each package in.

01 · See clearly

Diagnostics first. Decisions start from what's true, not what's loudest — and the findings report becomes the scope for everything after it.

Division Science Program Snapshot

~4–6 weeks · document review, site visits, structured teacher voice

A structured diagnostic of your science program: instructional materials, PD history, assessment data, SOL alignment, and what your teachers actually say they need.

Built to be buyable

Good PD dies in procurement more often than in classrooms. These engagements are designed for how Virginia divisions actually fund and approve outside help.

Meets the federal bar

Designed to the ESSA definition of professional development — sustained, job-embedded, collaborative, classroom-focused — so Title II-A funds can defensibly pay for it.

Counts for your teachers

Attendance and hour documentation delivered in a form your licensure office can approve in advance, so the work banks toward teachers' license-renewal points.

Sized to buy simply

Engagements scoped to fit a division's small-purchase authority for professional services — most won't require a formal RFP. [CONFIRM: thresholds vary by division]

Common funding lines divisions use for this work: Title II-A, Title IV-A, Title I schoolwide funds (school-level), and state grant programs as available.
