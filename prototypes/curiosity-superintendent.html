Where divisions put us to work

Science achievement and accreditation readiness

Instruction aligned to the Science SOL and its practices — so performance reflects teaching quality, not test-prep triage.

A defensible professional-development investment

A multi-year PD plan with a clear rationale you can present to your board, in place of one-off workshop spending.

Teacher growth and retention

Science teachers who get meaningful, subject-specific support are teachers who stay. Generic PD reaches no one.

Consistency across schools

Every student in the division deserves the same quality of science instruction — not just the schools with a strong department chair.

The engagement model

Three phases, scoped to your division's size and calendar.

PHASE 01

Assess

A structured review of your science program: instructional materials, PD history, SOL alignment, and what your teachers and coordinators say they need.

PHASE 02

Plan

A multi-year professional-development roadmap and resource plan, built with your instructional leadership and ready to defend line by line.

PHASE 03

Build

Direct work with teacher teams and science leads to put the plan into classrooms — so capacity stays in the division after the engagement ends.
